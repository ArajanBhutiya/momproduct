FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

Our launch packages start at ₹2,999 for a one-page website, ₹5,999 for up to five pages and ₹9,999 for roughly 8–10 pages. Domain, hosting and premium tools are charged separately. See the pricing page.

How long does it take?

Standard packages are delivered within 24 hours of us receiving your content, your confirmed quotation and the advance payment. Larger or custom projects get their own timeline in the quotation.

What does “24-hour delivery” mean?

The 24 hours start once we have everything we need: your text, logo and photos, the confirmed quotation and the advance payment. Our standard packages are built to that timeline. If your project needs more, we say so in the quotation before you pay.

How many revisions are included?

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds, shown on the pricing page. Larger changes beyond the agreed scope are quoted separately.

Do I need to buy hosting and a domain?

Yes — a domain name and hosting are needed for any website. We can guide you through choosing and setting them up. Their costs are separate and are paid to the provider or quoted transparently.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes. Optional maintenance covers updates and small changes. We will explain the options and cost before you decide.

Who owns the website?

Once the project is paid in full, you own the website content and your domain. Details are set out in our Terms of Service.
